| Year | Change | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 2003 | Teams must interview at least one minority candidate for head-coach jobs. When it began there had been only a handful of minority head coaches since Fritz Pollard in the 1920s | [Wikipedia: Rooney Rule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rooney_Rule) |
| 2009 | Extended to all senior football-operations jobs (from June 15, 2009) | same |
| 2018 / 2020 | Only 2 Black head coaches (end of 2018); 3 in 2020, the same as in 2003 | same |
| 2022 | Brian Flores sues the NFL and three teams over sham interviews (Feb 1). From the 2022 season every team must have a minority or a woman on its offensive coaching staff | same; [Wikipedia: Brian Flores](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brian_Flores) |

## 7. Safety rule changes (summary; detail in 06)

- 2009: return-to-play rule after concussions. 2013: independent neurologists on sidelines. Later: helmet-contact (lowering the head) rule, defenceless-player protections, kickoff redesigns (2023–2025 "dynamic kickoff") and Guardian Caps in practice (2022). Sources: [Wikipedia: Concussions in American football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concussions_in_American_football), [NFL 2025 injury data](https://www.nfl.com/playerhealthandsafety/health-and-wellness/injury-data/2025-season-key-takeaways).

## 8. Tax-exempt status

- The league office was a 501(c)(6) trade association. It gave the status up in 2015 after criticism in Congress, and Goodell called the issue a "distraction". The change cost about $10M a year. The clubs had always paid tax. Source: [Wikipedia: NFL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Football_League).
