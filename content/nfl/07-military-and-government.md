# 07 — Military and government

Compiled 2026-11-05. **[unconfirmed]** = search snippet only or a single weak source.

> **Note:** the NFL's patriotic and military ties are central to its US identity, but they don't translate abroad. The league's own former CMO warned that this patriotism can come across as "political in nature" overseas ([Yahoo Sports, Aug 2026](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/nfl-previous-push-europe-lost-070000673.html)).

## 2. Patriotic presentation

- Slate (2009) dates the military framing of the game to Rozelle, who made "a conscious effort" to tie the Super Bowl to patriotism. Military flyovers began at Super Bowl II, patriotic halftime shows at Super Bowl III, and the peaks came around the 1991 Gulf War and 2002 after 9/11. Source: [Slate](https://slate.com/culture/2009/11/how-the-nfl-became-the-american-war-game.html).

## 4. Salute to Service

- Started in 2011. Every November, teams wear camouflage gear and run military tributes. Over $80M raised for military charities: the Bob Woodruff Foundation, Pat Tillman Foundation, TAPS, USO and Wounded Warrior Project. Source: [NFL.com Salute to Service](https://www.nfl.com/causes/salute/).
