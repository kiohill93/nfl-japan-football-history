## 2. CTE research results (Boston University brain bank)

| Year published | Former NFL players with CTE | Share | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2014 | 76 of 79 | 96% | Concussions article |
| 2017 (*JAMA*) | 110 of 111 | 99% | same |
| 2026 (*BMJ*) | 315 of 338 | 93.2% | same |

These are donated brains, often from families who already suspected disease, so they don't measure how common CTE is across all players. [Wikipedia: CTE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chronic_traumatic_encephalopathy) explains that diagnosis is only possible after death.

## 4. Equipment and rule changes

| Year | Change | Effect | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2010 | Kickoff moved to the 35-yard line (described in the source as the league's most drastic head-injury step); three-man wedge banned | Fewer returns | Concussions article |
| 2010s–2024 | Helmet lab testing; players told not to wear poorly rated models; 12 new models approved for 2024 | Over 98% of players now wear top-rated helmets | Concussions article; NFL 2025 injury data |
| 2022 | Guardian Caps (soft shells over helmets) required for linemen, tight ends and linebackers in preseason practice | **Concussions down 54% (raw) / 62% (adjusted)** in a peer-reviewed study | [PubMed 40746051, *Am J Sports Med* 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40746051/) |
| 2024 | Guardian Caps allowed in games | Optional | [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/story/_/id/40031598/nfl-allow-guardian-caps-helmets-regular-season) |
| 2024–25 | "Dynamic kickoff" redesign | 2025: returns more than doubled (+1,157 returned kickoffs). Injury rate per play fell (lower-body injuries −35%, ACL tears −25%), but kickoff concussions rose from 8 to 35 because there were many more returns | NFL 2025 injury data |

## 5. Retired players' benefits and health studies

- **Pensions:** the 2020 CBA cut the vesting requirement to 3 credited seasons and raised benefits for older retirees ([2020 CBA term sheet](https://www.nfl.com/news/full-term-sheet-of-proposed-collective-bargaining-agreement-0ap3000001102608)). Detailed pension amounts are **[not collected]**.
- **Player benefits** cost over $70M per club in 2025 ([Sportico](https://www.sportico.com/leagues/football/2026/nfl-2025-national-revenue-per-team-1234939659/)).
- **Disability:** Webster's case is the best-known example. The retirement board paid his estate about $1.8M after a court fight (Mike Webster article).
- **Studies:** the Harvard Football Players Health Study (funded through the NFLPA) has published reports on drugs, care and more; its PDF chapter was seen in search results but **not opened**. The settlement's Baseline Assessment Program has run 17,542 exams.
- **ALS:** former Saints player Steve Gleason (diagnosed 2011) received the Congressional Gold Medal in 2019 ([Wikipedia: Steve Gleason](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Gleason)).
