# 08 — Culture and society

Compiled 2026-11-05. **[unconfirmed]** = search snippet only or a single weak source.

## 1. Integration

| Year | Event | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1920–26 | Nine Black players in the league, including Fritz Pollard, who became the first Black head coach in 1921 | [Wikipedia: History of the NFL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_the_National_Football_League); [Wikipedia: Fritz Pollard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fritz_Pollard) |
| 1927 | No Black players | History of the NFL |
| 1933 | Two Black players (Joe Lillard, Ray Kemp) | same |
| 1934–45 | An unwritten colour line: no Black players for 12 seasons | same |
| 1946 | Los Angeles Rams sign Kenny Washington (first Black player to sign in the modern era) and Woody Strode. The public LA Coliseum's lease made integration a condition. In the rival AAFC, the Browns sign Marion Motley and Bill Willis the same year, a year before Jackie Robinson's MLB debut | same; [Wikipedia: Kenny Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenny_Washington_(American_football)); [Wikipedia: Marion Motley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marion_Motley) |
| 1960s | The AFL recruits heavily from historically Black colleges. In 1969 the AFL champion Chiefs' roster was 45% Black vs 26% for the NFL's Vikings | History of the NFL |
| 1962 | Washington is the last team to integrate, under pressure from the Kennedy administration | same |
| 2003– | Rooney Rule for coaching and executive hiring (see 05) | [Wikipedia: Rooney Rule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rooney_Rule) |
| Today | Players are roughly 67–71% non-white | History of the NFL |

### Asian and Asian-American players

- First Asian-American players: Walter Achiu (1927) and Arthur Matsu (1928), per History of the NFL.
- Japanese-born and Japanese-American NFL players since then are covered on this site's Japan page (06, Military and US ties), not here.
