# 01 — NFL timeline, 1920 to November 2026

Compiled 2026-11-05. Facts rest on the sources linked in each section; **[unconfirmed]** marks anything seen only in a search snippet or a single weak source.

## 1. Founding era (1920–1945)

| Year | Event | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1920 | American Professional Football Association (APFA) founded in Canton, Ohio. Jim Thorpe named president, mostly as a figurehead while still playing for Canton. | [Wikipedia: NFL Commissioner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commissioner_of_the_NFL) |
| 1921 | Joe Carr becomes president; Green Bay Packers join and are caught using college players under false names (first test of league authority). | same |
| 1922 | APFA renamed National Football League. | [Wikipedia: National Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Football_League) |
| 1920–26 | Nine Black players appear in the league; none in 1927; two (Lillard, Kemp) in 1933; then an unwritten colour line, 1934–45. | [Wikipedia: History of the NFL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_the_National_Football_League) |
| 1926 | Red Grange's barnstorming pay (about $100K) shows the star-driven economics of the early pro game. | [Expensivity](https://www.expensivity.com/pro-football-salary-inflation/) |
| 1927–28 | First Asian-American players: Walter Achiu (1927) and Arthur Matsu (1928). | History of the NFL (Wikipedia) |
| 1933 | Steelers and Eagles founded (both still active). | NFL teams table, Wikipedia NFL |
| 1939–41 | Carr dies; Carl Storck president; Jan 17, 1941 title changed to "commissioner"; Elmer Layden first commissioner. | Wikipedia: NFL Commissioner |
| 1941–45 | 638 NFL men serve in WWII; 21 (NFL) or 23 (Pro Football Hall of Fame count) die. | see 07, Military and government |

## 2. Post-war growth and the rival leagues (1946–1969)

| Year | Event | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1946 | Bert Bell commissioner. Rams move to Los Angeles and sign Kenny Washington and Woody Strode (the LA Coliseum lease required integration). The rival AAFC's Browns sign Marion Motley and Bill Willis. | History of the NFL; [Wikipedia: Kenny Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenny_Washington_(American_football)) |
| 1950 | Browns, 49ers, Colts join from the AAFC. | NFL teams table |
| 1956 | NFL Players Association founded. | [Wikipedia: NFLPA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Football_League_Players_Association) |
| 1957 | *Radovich v. NFL*: Supreme Court rules football, unlike baseball, is subject to antitrust law. | [Wikipedia: Radovich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radovich_v._National_Football_League) |
| 1959 | Bell dies at a game (Oct 11, 1959). | Wikipedia: NFL Commissioner |
| 1960 | Pete Rozelle becomes commissioner. American Football League (AFL) starts play. Cowboys founded. | Wikipedia: NFL Commissioner; NFL teams table |
| 1961 | Sports Broadcasting Act lets leagues pool TV rights. Vikings join. | [Wikipedia: SBA 1961](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sports_Broadcasting_Act_of_1961) |
| 1963 | Paul Hornung and Alex Karras suspended for a season for betting on games. | [Wikipedia: Alex Karras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alex_Karras) |
| 1965 | Harris Poll: pro football overtakes baseball as Americans' favourite sport. Joe Namath signs with the AFL Jets for $427,000. | History of the NFL |
| 1966 | AFL–NFL merger announced June 8, 1966; Congress passes the antitrust exemption (PL 89-800). Falcons and (AFL) Dolphins join. | [Wikipedia: AFL–NFL merger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AFL%E2%80%93NFL_merger) |
| 1967 | First Super Bowl (Jan 15, 1967); Saints join. | [Wikipedia: Super Bowl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl) |
| 1968 | First collective bargaining agreement; Bengals join the AFL. | Wikipedia: NFLPA |
| 1969 | Jets win Super Bowl III, proving AFL parity. | [Wikipedia: Super Bowl III](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_III) |

## 3. The merged league and the TV era (1970–1989)

| Year | Event | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1970 | Full merger: 26 teams, AFC and NFC. *Monday Night Football* debuts on ABC. | [Awful Announcing](https://awfulannouncing.com/2015/nfl-tv-changed-dramatically-over-the-last-50-years.html) |
| 1976 | **First NFL game outside North America: Cardinals 20–10 Chargers, Korakuen Stadium, Tokyo, Aug 16, 1976** (the "Mainichi Star Bowl"). Seahawks and Buccaneers join. | [NFL.com](https://www.nfl.com/news/adventures-abroad-remembering-nfl-s-1976-foray-into-japan-0ap3000000739785), [Yahoo Sports](https://sports.yahoo.com/article/time-chargers-went-way-tokyo-100000702.html) |
| 1978 | First game in Mexico City (preseason). | [Wikipedia: American Bowl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Bowl) |
| 1982 | 57-day players' strike; 9-game season. | [Wikipedia: 1982 NFL strike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1982_NFL_strike) |
| 1983 | First London game (preseason, "Global Cup"). USFL starts play in spring. | Wikipedia: American Bowl; [Wikipedia: USFL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Football_League) |
| 1986 | USFL wins an antitrust verdict but is awarded $1 (trebled to $3); USFL folds. American Bowl series begins. | Wikipedia: USFL |
| 1987 | 24-day strike; three games played with replacement players. ESPN becomes the first cable partner. Steroid testing begins. | [Wikipedia: 1987 NFL strike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1987_NFL_strike); [Wikipedia: Doping in American football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doping_in_American_football) |
| 1989 | Paul Tagliabue succeeds Rozelle. American Bowl comes to the Tokyo Dome (Rams 16–49ers 13). | Wikipedia: NFL Commissioner; Wikipedia: American Bowl |

| 2005 | **First regular-season game outside the US: Cardinals–49ers, Mexico City, Oct 2, 2005 (103,467).** Last Japan game to date: Falcons 27–Colts 21, Tokyo Dome (preseason). | Wikipedia: American Bowl; [Japan Times 2015](https://www.japantimes.co.jp/sports/2015/02/04/more-sports/football/football-diehards-yearn-nfls-return-japan/) |

| Year | Event | Source |
|---|---|---|

| 2021 | 11-year media deals (2023–2033) worth over $100B; first sportsbook partners (Caesars, DraftKings, FanDuel). | [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2021/03/18/nfl-media-rights-deal-2023-2033-amazon-gets-exclusive-thursday-night.html); UC Law Review |

| 2024 | First game in Brazil (São Paulo); Netflix gets Christmas games; private-equity stakes allowed. | Wikipedia: NFL; [Quartz](https://qz.com/nfl-most-valuable-team-2026) |
| 2025 | Brazil game streamed free worldwide on YouTube. Super Bowl LIX sets the US TV record (127.7M average). Paul Tagliabue dies (Nov 9). | [Wikipedia: NFL International Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NFL_International_Series); Wikipedia: Super Bowl; Wikipedia: NFL Commissioner |
| 2026 | ESPN takes over NFL Network; NFL takes 10% of ESPN (closed about Feb 1). Record 9 international games, first in Melbourne, Rio de Janeiro and Paris. Salary cap passes $300M. Mahomes signs a record $64M-a-year deal (June). | [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/espn/story/_/id/47793895/regulators-ok-espn-purchase-nfl-network-redzone-rights-nfl-equity-stake); [NFL 2026 schedule](https://media.nfl.com/news-and-releases/international/nfl-unveils-2026-international-games-schedule); [NFL Ops cap](https://operations.nfl.com/calendar-events/nfl-free-agency/nfl-salary-cap) |

## 6. League size over time

| Year | Teams | What changed | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1960 | 13 NFL + 8 AFL | AFL begins; Cowboys | NFL teams table |
| 1970 | 26 | Merger complete | AFL–NFL merger |
| 1976 | 28 | Seahawks, Buccaneers | NFL teams table |
| 1995 | 30 | Panthers, Jaguars | same |
| 1999 | 31 | Browns restored | same |
| 2002 | 32 | Texans | same |

No expansion since 2002. Growth has instead come from relocations to bigger markets (LA, Las Vegas) and international games.
