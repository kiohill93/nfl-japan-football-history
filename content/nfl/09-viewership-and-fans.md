# 09 — Viewership and fans

Compiled 2026-11-05. **[unconfirmed]** = search snippet only or a single weak source. **[derived]** = my arithmetic from a cited source.

> **Measurement note:** from the 2025 season Nielsen counts more out-of-home and streaming viewing ("Big Data + Panel"). That helped the 2025 records, so recent numbers are not fully comparable with older years ([Sportico, Jan 2026](https://www.sportico.com/business/media/2026/nfl-tv-ratings-high-nielsen-methodology-1234880431/)). The NFL complained to Nielsen about further changes in August 2026 **[unconfirmed: Yahoo snippet]**.

- Football has been first in all 23 Gallup readings since 1972, averaging 38% in season and 32% out of season.
- **Age gap (2026):** 20% of 18–34-year-olds pick football, against 30% of 35–54 and 38% of 55+. 23% of young adults have no favourite sport.
- **Income (2026):** upper-income 37%, middle 32%, lower 25%.
- **Harris Poll:** pro football became America's favourite sport in 1965, and stood at 30% in 2008 ([History of the NFL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_the_National_Football_League)).

**Fan counts:** estimates vary with definitions. One commercial estimate puts US NFL fans at 143.8M with 45.1% women **[unconfirmed: Rascasse snippet]**. S&P Kagan (Q1 2024) found 48% of US internet adults watch NFL games **[snippet]**.

## 6. Who watches: demographics

| Measure | Traditional TV viewers | Streaming viewers | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Median age | 54 | 43 | [Spectrum Reach / Scarborough 2025](https://www.spectrumreach.com/sites/default/files/2026-06/nfl-fan-profile.pdf) |
| Male / female | 56% / **44%** | 62% / **38%** | same |
| Age 18–49 share | 41% | 61% | same |
| Any college | 67% | 69% | same |
| Higher than median household income | +20% | +28% | same |

- **Women:** about 44% of NFL TV viewers (Scarborough above). The common "47% of fans are women" line was not found in an opened source.
- **Hispanic / Latino fans:** the NFL's fastest-growing US fan group ([NPR, Dec 2024](https://www.npr.org/2024/12/21/g-s1-38443/latinos-growing-fanbase-nfl)). Spanish-language Super Bowl viewing reached 3.3M in 2026 (table above). Under 15% of US NFL fans are Black **[snippet: Statista]**.
- **Gen Z / young adults:** the weak spot. Only 20% of 18–34s name football their favourite (Gallup 2026). Streaming audiences are younger (median 43). Netflix, YouTube creators (MrBeast was on the 2025 YouTube game) and Bad Bunny at Super Bowl LX are aimed at this group.
