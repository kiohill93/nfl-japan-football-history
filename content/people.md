# Important people: the NFL and football in Japan

Compiled 2026-11-05 from the NFL and Japan research on this site. One doc for learning the major names behind both stories.

**How to read it**
- **Part 1** covers the NFL's own story: founders, commissioners, owners, union leaders, media, players, coaches, health and safety, and the international side. "File" numbers in Part 1 point to the numbered sections of the NFL history page.
- **Part 2** covers football in Japan: founders, federation and league leaders, coaches, players, media and culture, business, and NFL people working on Japan today. "File" numbers in Part 2 point to the numbered sections of the Japan page.
- Each part starts with "If you only learn these 15".
- **Name order:** Part 1 writes Japanese names given name first (Takayuki Hioki); Part 2 uses family name first, as in Japan (Hioki Takayuki), with kanji.
- **Markers:** **[unconfirmed]**, **[?]** = not confirmed in an opened source; **[conflict]** = sources disagree; **[JA]**/**[EN]** = Japanese or English source.

**People in both stories:** Roger Goodell, Brett Gosper, Frank Takahashi (promoter of the 1976 Tokyo game), Takayuki Hioki (Sports Branding Japan, the NFL's Japan liaison office), and the Rams (Stan Kroenke; Stefanie Chen, head of Rams international).
