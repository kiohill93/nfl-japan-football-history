**Two flag bodies:** JAFA runs the national flag team and the Olympic route; JFFO runs separate school-oriented programs.

## 4. Governance weaknesses (as reported)

- **Conflicts of interest:** the league chair was also a club representative during a dispute about that club (Gendai).
- **Thin staffing:** the X League has 11 staff and a volunteer board of about 15, mostly nominated by clubs (Gendai).
- **Closed culture:** Nishimura describes the sport as an insular "football village" that rarely brings in outsiders (Gendai).
- **Light penalties:** a boycott drew a ¥1M fine (Gendai).
- **Women in leadership:** 2 of 23 JAFA directors are women (JAFA FY2025 report).
