# 03 — Economics and salaries

Research date: 2026-11-05. **[JA]** Japanese source, **[EN]** English source, **[?]** unconfirmed, **[conflict]** sources disagree.

## 1. The basic model: company employees who play

Japanese top-level football has always run on the corporate sports model: players are company employees (or hold other jobs) and play after work. The 2026 licence announcement said 99% of X League players have another job (Nikkan Sports, 2025-08-04). A 1989 survey of college all-star players found 54.7% were joining companies with a corporate-league team and 14.7% companies with associate or planned teams (JAFA year page 1989, citing *American Football Magazine*, March 1990). In other words, the sport was a hiring channel for big employers in the bubble years.

The federation depends on public sport money (JSC lottery grants and JOC funds, which grew after flag football joined the Olympics) more than on commercial income. Sponsorship and TV together bring in only about ¥7.5M.
