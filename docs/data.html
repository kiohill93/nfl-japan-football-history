Data honesty: these are research notes compiled from public sources, not official NFL or JAFA data. Numbers are copied as the sources give them. Values that are unconfirmed, conflicting or seen only in a search snippet stay in the text and in the CSV note columns, and are left out of the charts. Missing years are missing, not zero.
